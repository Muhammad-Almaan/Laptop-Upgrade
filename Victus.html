The Problem

My Current Laptop Is a Barrier

Every day, my weak hardware creates real friction that slows down learning, kills focus, and limits what I can build. These are not complaints — they are documented limitations with measurable consequences.

🐌

Severe RAM Bottleneck

4 GB RAM is below the minimum for modern development. Opening VS Code, a browser with a few tabs, and Node.js simultaneously causes constant freezing and crashes.

🔥

CPU Cannot Keep Up

The Intel i3 struggles with compilation, running local servers, or any CPU-intensive task. Build times stretch from seconds to minutes, breaking concentration and flow.

📉

No GPU — No AI/ML Work

Integrated graphics cannot accelerate machine learning tasks, data visualisation, or even smooth video rendering. Entire career pathways are locked behind this limitation.

⏳

Time Lost Daily

Boot times, app loading, and browser lag together waste 30–60 minutes every day. Over a year, that is hundreds of hours of lost productive time.
